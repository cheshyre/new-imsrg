## 5. Old-code bugs and quirks fixed (details in `30-testing-and-validation.md` §6)

* **D35-1:** imsrg++ `TCM_Op` drops two-body entries below 1e-7 (effect ≤ 2.4e-15 MeV in the tests);
  we keep all entries. **D35-2:** `Rp2_corrected_Op` folds the spin-orbit correction into Rp2
  (Rp2 shifts by ~9e-5 fm² at O16); Rp2 is without it now and `Rso` is its own operator (charge
  radius = Rp2 + Rso/Z + nucleon-size terms, M8-Q20). Both validated with the patched imsrg-devel
  build.
* imsrg++'s adaptive `flow` integrates a copy of the solver, so s restarts at 0 in stage 2; here s
  continues (M8-Q40; identical when stage 1 converged).
* From the Opus flow review: the stage-2 solver options were never applied; NaN norms ended as
  "smax reached" (now Diverged, and BCH stops on non-finite nested commutators); option validation
  moved into the library (`InputError`).
* `SixJTable` returned 0 for a three-body J, a silent wrong result (now throws on the host, NaN on
  the device); the new `SixJ3Table` covers the 3B family.
* Findings without a result change: imsrg++ keeps 36 kets that vanish under antisymmetrization at
  emax 3; `comm223ss` has no bug in the tested configurations; the `Eta_matrix_nnnn` sign comment
  "this is a guess" (M10-Q22).
* Not reproduced on purpose: imsrg++'s occupation skip thresholds in the factorized term
  (`30` §6 M10-3f2-1), its early return in `comm223ss` (M10-Q43), the ODE monitor printout.
* (RESULT PENDING: imsrg++'s IMSRG(3) bugs of `14` §1 found and fixed by the N7 work and the
